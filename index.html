FAQ

Before you join.

What is Drop Chain?

Drop Chain is a lifting-belt attachment in development for weighted dips and pull-ups. The prototype combines reinforced nylon webbing, D-rings, carabiners, and a chain. It is not yet available to buy.

How is it different from a separate dip belt?

A separate dip belt provides its own belt and loading attachment. Drop Chain is being designed as an attachment for a compatible lifting belt you already own. Compatibility and load capacity are still being tested; it is not confirmed to work with every belt.

Compatibility is still being validated. The final supported belt types will be published before launch.
