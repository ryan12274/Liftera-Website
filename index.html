FAQ

Before you join.

What is Drop Chain?

Drop Chain is an attachment for adding weight to dips and pull-ups using a lifting belt you already own. It combines nylon webbing, connection hardware, and a chain. See the prototype in action.

How is it different from a dip belt?

A dip belt includes its own belt. Drop Chain adds a loading attachment to your existing compatible lifting belt, so you can keep using your current belt.

Will it fit my lifting belt?

Drop Chain fits most standard lifting belts. Not sure about yours? Email Liftera.mn@yahoo.com with your belt model or a photo, and we can help check the fit.

How much weight has it been tested with?

Our prototype has been load-tested at 200 lb. This is the load we have tested so far, not a finalized maximum load rating. Testing is ongoing.

What is included?

Just the Drop Chain attachment. A lifting belt and weight plates are not included.

Can I buy through VE?

Yes. Buy Drop Chain through our VE checkout. This is part of the Virtual Enterprises educational marketplace. The physical product is still a prototype, and its design, materials, and specifications may change. Joining our email waitlist is separate and does not place an order.

What happens when I join the waitlist?

You will receive Drop Chain development and launch updates by email. Joining is free and does not reserve a product or require payment. You can leave the list at any time by emailing us.
