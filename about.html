OUR STORY

From an idea to a business.

LIFTERA was created through our Virtual Enterprise program at St. Michael-Albertville High School. The company gives students the opportunity to work through the process of building a business, from product development and branding to technology, marketing, operations, and sales.

Our first product, Drop Chain, came from a simple training problem: adding weight to dips and pull-ups can require another piece of equipment when a lifter may already have a belt that works. We are developing LIFTERA around the idea that useful training equipment should solve a problem without making the setup more complicated.
