What this policy covers

Paid preorders for the physical Drop Chain product through Stripe. Virtual Enterprises transactions are separate educational purchases and do not include physical delivery.

Price, payment & estimated shipment

Drop Chain costs $35 USD, plus shipping and any applicable taxes. We collect full payment when you preorder. The complete amount is shown before you pay.

We estimate that preorders will ship in mid-December 2026. This is an estimated shipment window, not a delivery date. Transit time depends on the carrier and destination.

We currently plan to ship to the lower 48 U.S. states and Washington, D.C. Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, military addresses, and international destinations are not supported for this launch.

Your order includes one Drop Chain attachment. A lifting belt and weight plates are not included. Product images show the prototype; final design, materials, and specifications may change. If a change materially affects your order, we will contact you before shipment and offer the option to cancel for a full refund.

Cancel before shipment

You may cancel your preorder any time before it is handed to the carrier. We will provide a full refund of the amount paid for the unshipped order, including any shipping charges and taxes. No cancellation or restocking fee will apply.

Email Liftera.mn@yahoo.com from the email address used for the order. Include your order number and name so we can identify it. Do not send card numbers or banking details.

How refunds work

Refunds will go back to the original payment method. We will confirm when a refund has been initiated. The time it takes to appear in your account depends on your bank or payment provider.

If shipping is delayed

If we cannot meet the stated shipping timeframe, we will contact you with the revised estimate, if known, and offer a choice: agree to wait or cancel for a full refund.

We will ask for your agreement to the delay and state a response deadline. If you choose not to wait, or do not agree by that deadline, we will cancel the unshipped order and refund it in full.

If LIFTERA cancels an order

If we cannot fulfill your preorder, we will notify you and provide a full refund for the unshipped order.

Returns after delivery

Contact us within 30 days after delivery to request a return of an unused item. Include your order reference and name, and wait for the return instructions and address before mailing it back.

The item must be unused and returned with its included parts. You pay return shipping. Once we receive and inspect an eligible return, we will refund the returned product’s purchase price to the original payment method. Original outbound shipping charges are not refunded for a change-of-mind return.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

Stop using the item and email Liftera.mn@yahoo.com with your order reference and a description of the issue. Photos may help us assess it. We will work with you on an appropriate replacement or refund and cover necessary return shipping for a confirmed defective, damaged or incorrect item.

Questions before ordering?

Email Liftera.mn@yahoo.com. Keep your order reference for any cancellation, return or shipping question. Never send card numbers or banking information by email.
