LIFTERA / DRAFT FOR REVIEW

Preorder cancellations
& refunds.

Proposed terms. Not yet in effect.

Physical preorders are not open. The Stripe checkout on preview is in test mode. This draft is for the LIFTERA team to review before real orders begin.

Draft updated November 13, 2026

What this policy would cover

Future paid preorders for the physical Drop Chain product through Stripe. Virtual Enterprises transactions are separate educational purchases and do not include physical delivery.

Cancel before shipment

You may cancel your preorder any time before it is handed to the carrier. We will provide a full refund of the amount paid for the unshipped order, including any shipping charges and taxes. No cancellation or restocking fee will apply.

Email Liftera.mn@yahoo.com from the email address used for the order. Include your order number and name so we can identify it. Do not send card numbers or banking details.

How refunds work

Refunds will go back to the original payment method. We will confirm when a refund has been initiated. The time it takes to appear in your account depends on your bank or payment provider.

If shipping is delayed

If we cannot meet the stated shipping timeframe, we will contact you with the revised estimate, if known, and offer a choice: agree to wait or cancel for a full refund.

We will ask for your agreement to the delay and state a response deadline. If you choose not to wait, or do not agree by that deadline, we will cancel the unshipped order and refund it in full.

If LIFTERA cancels an order

If we cannot fulfill your preorder, we will notify you and provide a full refund for the unshipped order.

After shipment

Once the order has been handed to the carrier, it can no longer be canceled before shipping. A separate policy for returns, damaged items, and product issues must be finalized and published before real preorders open.

Still to decide before launch

The team still needs to confirm the shipping timeframe and charges, when real payments will be collected, refund-processing procedures, and the post-delivery return policy. No shipping date or fee is promised by this draft.

Draft reference: FTC guidance on shipping delays and refunds ↗.
