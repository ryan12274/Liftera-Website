What the form collects

When you join, we collect your email address through our website signup form.

How it is used

Your email is used for Drop Chain launch updates, availability, and confirmed product details. Signing up does not place an order, take payment, or reserve a product.

Where submissions go

Cloudflare Pages hosts this website. A previous version was hosted on Netlify. New waitlist submissions are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database accessible to the LIFTERA team. Cloudflare Turnstile checks submissions to reduce spam. We store your email address, signup date, consent version, and the website where you signed up. Earlier submissions may remain in Brevo or Netlify Forms until transferred or deleted. These providers may process technical request information to operate and protect their services.

Preorders and payment information

When paid preorders open, Stripe will handle checkout and collect the information needed to process and fulfill your order, including your name, email address, billing and shipping address, and payment details. LIFTERA does not receive or store your full card number or CVC.

LIFTERA uses order information for payment confirmation, shipping, cancellations, refunds and support. A purchase does not automatically sign you up for marketing emails. Stripe may send payment and refund receipts.

Our Cloudflare order records contain an order reference, Stripe session and payment identifiers, amounts paid or refunded, order and fulfillment status, the shipping estimate and the version of the preorder terms. Customer contact and address details remain in Stripe and are accessible to the authorized LIFTERA team for fulfillment and support. Test records are kept separate from real orders.

A signed, secure checkout cookie helps the website resume and verify your checkout for up to 24 hours. Stripe may use its own cookies and services to process payments and prevent fraud. See Stripe’s privacy policy ↗.

We keep order records as needed for fulfillment, refunds, disputes and recordkeeping. Email us about access or deletion; some transaction records may need to be retained. Preview checkout uses Stripe test mode and is intended for fictitious details only.

Customer reviews

If you submit a review, we collect your chosen display name, rating, written feedback, whether you used the prototype or physical product, and your consent to publication. Approved reviews display those details publicly. Do not put email addresses, phone numbers or other private information in your review.

Reviews are stored in Cloudflare D1 and checked by LIFTERA before publication. Turnstile checks submissions for spam. We store a hashed request identifier to limit repeat submissions; raw IP addresses are not stored in the review table. A review does not subscribe you to marketing emails. Contact us below to request a correction or removal.

Leave the list or ask a question

To stop updates, contact us at the address below. Future marketing emails will include an unsubscribe option. To request deletion of your waitlist submission, email Liftera.mn@yahoo.com from the address you signed up with. You can also contact us there with privacy questions.
