What the form collects

When you join, we collect your email address and, when provided in the link you followed, a campaign source and campaign name. These help us understand which links bring people to the waitlist. Please do not include personal information in campaign links.

How it is used

Your email is used for Drop Chain launch updates, availability, and confirmed product details. Signing up does not place an order, take payment, or reserve a product.

Where submissions go

The website and signup form use Netlify. Form submissions are processed and stored through Netlify Forms, where the LIFTERA team can manage the list. Hosting providers may also process technical request information to operate and protect their services.

Leave the list or ask a question

To stop updates or request deletion of your waitlist submission, email Liftera.mn@yahoo.com from the address you signed up with. You can also contact us there with privacy questions.
