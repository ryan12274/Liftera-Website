What the form collects

When you join, we collect your email address through our website signup form.

How it is used

Your email is used for Drop Chain launch updates, availability, and confirmed product details. Signing up does not place an order, take payment, or reserve a product.

Where submissions go

Cloudflare Pages hosts this website. A previous version was hosted on Netlify. New waitlist submissions are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database accessible to the LIFTERA team. Cloudflare Turnstile checks submissions to reduce spam. We store your email address, signup date, consent version, and the website where you signed up. Earlier submissions may remain in Brevo or Netlify Forms until transferred or deleted. These providers may process technical request information to operate and protect their services.

Leave the list or ask a question

To stop updates, contact us at the address below. Future marketing emails will include an unsubscribe option. To request deletion of your waitlist submission, email Liftera.mn@yahoo.com from the address you signed up with. You can also contact us there with privacy questions.
