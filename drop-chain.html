9 IN REINFORCED WEBBING / 3 FT CHAIN

THE IDEA

Don't replace your belt.
Build on it.

Drop Chain is designed to sit over your existing weight belt and add the connection points you need for weighted calisthenics. One simple piece of gear instead of another complete belt system.

DETAILS

Simple by design.

01

Reinforced nylon

9-inch reinforced nylon webbing distributes the load across your existing belt.

02

D-ring connectors

Integrated D-rings provide a clean attachment point on each side.

03

Heavy-duty chain

Approximately 3 feet of 3/16-inch chain gives you room to position the load where you need it.

04

Carabiner connection

Clip in, add your weight, and get to the movement without unnecessary setup.

MADE FOR

DIPS.
PULL-UPS.
PROGRESSION.

Whether you're adding your first plate or chasing another PR, Drop Chain keeps the setup familiar and focused.

Use it with your existing belt and the plates you already have. No proprietary loading system. No unnecessary hardware.
