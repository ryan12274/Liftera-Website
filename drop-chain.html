THE CONCEPT

One attachment.
Your setup stays yours.

Drop Chain is designed as the link between a belt you already trust and the loading hardware you need for weighted calisthenics.

VALIDATION

Test it before we sell it.

Because this product is intended to suspend loaded weight, we will publish the tested maximum load, hardware ratings, and supported belt types before selling it. Do not use the concept render or an unvalidated prototype as a rated lifting product.

USE CASE

For the days when bodyweight stops being enough.

Weighted dips and pull-ups are the starting point. The goal is a setup that feels familiar, packs small, and lets you focus on the movement instead of the equipment.
