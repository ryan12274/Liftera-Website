## Current priorities

- Choose whether to keep Stripe or move online sales to Shopify before changing payment infrastructure.
- Confirm packed weight, dimensions, shipping origin, carrier, and customer shipping charge.
- Finish payment, refund, receipt, policy, and fulfillment checks before accepting real preorders.
- Keep preview records separate from production customers.

Follow the [contribution workflow](../CONTRIBUTING.md) when changing the site.
